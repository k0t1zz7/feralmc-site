How the 10 lives work

One number decides everything. Check it any time in game with /lives.

10

Lives per season

Every death costs one life. When the last one is gone, you are banned for the rest of the season.

1h

Respawn protection

After each respawn nobody can kill you or open your chests for one hour. Use it to get your gear back or run.

0

Wipes

The world is permanent for the whole season. Bases, stashes and grudges stay exactly where you left them.

Leaving the game in a fight counts as a death. Disconnecting does not save you.

365

Days per season

Rules

Few rules, enforced. Everything not listed here is part of the game.

  1. Licensed accounts only.Once your lives are gone, a free account cannot bring you back.
  2. No movement cheats.Fly, speed and similar hacks are blocked by the anti-cheat and punished.
  3. No crash exploits or lag machines.Anything built to slow down or take down the server gets you banned.
  4. Everything else is allowed.Raiding, griefing, trapping, base hunting and wars between players. We do not roll back player actions.
  5. Admin decisions on lives are final.Restoring lives happens only for a reason, and the reason is recorded.

FAQ

The questions new players ask first.

Which version and edition do I need?

Minecraft Java Edition 26.3 with a licensed account. Bedrock is not supported.

What happens after my 10th death?

You are banned until the next season starts. Your lives reset to 10 when the new season begins.

Does the server wipe?

No wipes during a season. The current plan is one season of one full year, starting January 1, 2027.

Is hacking allowed?

Movement hacks and anything that crashes or lags the server are banned. Other client-side tools are up to the players.

Can I get lives back?

Only server admins can restore lives, and only with a reason, for example a confirmed crash or a bug. Dying to another player is not a reason.

Where is the server and is it protected?

It runs in Frankfurt, Germany, behind DDoS protection, with automatic backups several times a day.

Where do I ask for help or suggest ideas?

In our Discord server.
